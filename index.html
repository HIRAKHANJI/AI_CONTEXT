📜 The Admission Protocol

Nothing gets stocked unless it passes all five gates. The default answer is reject — things earn their way in. This is how we beat shiny-object syndrome.

G1
💸 Free

Free forever for a solo dev (a genuinely usable, non-expiring free tier counts). Paid-only → Showroom.

G2
🛠️ Maintained

Active in the last ~12 months, versioned, not an abandoned experiment.

G3
🛡️ Safe

Open-source or trusted vendor; you can see what data/permissions it touches; no history of malice.

G4
🎯 Real job

Solves a concrete need in one of the 14 departments — not just “neat.”

G5
🪟 Fits you

Works on Windows + Claude Code with reasonable setup and low babysitting.

Then it's scored (value · maturity · setup ease · fit), an overlap penalty is applied (one job, one tool), and it lands in a tier. Full rules, scoring rubric and decision flowchart live in docs/PROTOCOL.md.
